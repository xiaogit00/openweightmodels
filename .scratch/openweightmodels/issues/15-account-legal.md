# 15: Account lifecycle and legal surface

**What to build:** The product has the minimum legal and account-lifecycle surface required for public use: Terms and Privacy pages, a data export, account deletion with a defined content policy, and a stated retention policy. Users can understand and exercise control over their data without an admin.

**Blocked by:** 07 — Sign in with email magic links.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Terms of Service and Privacy Policy pages are linked from the public shell and describe the actual data collected and processed.
- [ ] A signed-in user can export their account data in a machine-readable format.
- [ ] A signed-in user can delete their account after an explicit confirmation; the email and session are removed.
- [ ] Deletion defines what happens to authored Comparisons, comments and Annotations; the chosen policy is implemented consistently and explained to the user.
- [ ] The retention policy for uploaded Base outputs, generated Responses and activity data is documented.
- [ ] Deleted-account content does not leave orphaned personal data or broken public pages.
- [ ] The legal copy is clearly marked for owner/legal review rather than presented as final without review.
